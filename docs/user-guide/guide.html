Getting started

An account takes a minute. Invite links bring you straight back to the league or contest.

Create your account

From the app link, or an invite a friend sent you.
  1. Open the link and tap Create one (from an invite, the form opens on its own).
  2. Enter your name, a display name (what friends see on the table), your email and a password of 8+ characters.
  3. Tap Create account. You are signed in at once.

Forgot your password?

There is no email from the app, so the admins help.
  1. On sign-in tap Forgot password?, enter your email, tap Ask for a reset.
  2. An admin sends you a temporary password (usually on WhatsApp).
  3. Sign in with it; the app asks you to choose your own first.

How a league works

A season-long competition among friends, for a tournament such as IPL 2026.

The deal

Set by the organizer when the league is created.

Balance

  • You are in every match automatically once your place is confirmed. You only make your pick.
  • Each match is a small pot. Everyone's ₹100 goes in; those who picked the winner share it.
  • The table ranks everyone by net profit: what you've won minus what your entries cost so far.
  • At the end the organizer hands you back your final balance = buy-in + net profit. Nobody pays more than the buy-in.
ExampleYou win ₹2,600 over the season and your entries cost ₹2,000: net profit +₹600, so you get back ₹2,600. Someone at −₹500 gets back ₹1,500.

Joining and playing

  1. Open the invite: the link, or Leagues → enter the invite code. You see the price and deadlines.
  2. Tap Ask to join before "Joining closes". The organizer is told.
  3. Pay the buy-in as the league's "How to pay" says, before Payment due. Unpaid places are released automatically.
  4. The organizer marks Payment received; you get an alert and you're Confirmed.
  5. Pick: open a match (league → Contests) and tap the team you think will win. Change it any time until the start; then picks lock.
  6. Follow the Table, and Share on WhatsApp to your group.
Good to know: other players' picks stay hidden until the match starts. No pick still counts as an entry, and can't win. Nobody joins once the first match has started.

Dugout XI5 – 6 · Contests and results

One-off contests

A single match with its own entry fee, outside any league. It settles as soon as the result is in.
  1. Open it from the link a friend shared, or Contests → enter its number (for example #9) → Open contest.
  2. Tap Ask to join before "Joining closes". You can Withdraw my request while it waits.
  3. Once accepted, pay the entry before "Payment due", or your place is released.
  4. Pick the winner before the match starts.
  5. After the result, the Money tab shows who is owed what. The organizer pays out and marks each one Paid out.

A contest with fewer than 2 confirmed players at its start is cancelled, and anyone who paid is refunded.

Running a league

Anyone can. Whoever creates a league is its organizer, and plays in it too (your own buy-in counts as paid).
  1. Create it. Leagues → Start a league: name, tournament, amount per match, number of matches, joining and payment dates, and how to pay ("UPI to name@okbank").
  2. Open it. Open for joining, or it opens on its own at the "joining opens" time.
  3. Invite people from the league page: invite code, Copy invite link or Share on WhatsApp.
  4. Let people in (Players): Accept, or Accept as paid if you have their money; otherwise Payment received when it arrives. You can Decline or Remove.
  5. Add matches (Contests → Add a contest): two teams and a start time after the payment deadline. Every confirmed player is entered.
  6. Enter each result: match → Result → winner (or no result) → Work out the payouts shows who wins what → Confirm this result.
  7. End the season with End the league: every balance is frozen.
  8. Settle up (Settle up): hand each player back the amount To hand back, tap Mark settled, then Close the league.
TipsAdd matches a few days ahead so players get pick reminders. Use Reschedule on a match if its start moves (everyone is told). Download the league's Excel after each week.

One-off contests

  1. Contests → Create a contest: tournament, teams, start time, entry, joining and payment deadlines, how to pay.
  2. Share it from the Invite players box: Share on WhatsApp, Copy invite link or the contest number.
  3. Players: Accept requests and tap Payment received as money arrives.
  4. After the match: Work out the payouts → Confirm this result. Then Money: send each winning or refund and tap Mark as paid.

Questions and answers

Does the app take or hold my money?

No. All money goes directly between you and the organizer. The app only records it.

I missed the joining or payment deadline.

Ask the organizer: they can change the dates until the league starts. Once the first match has started nobody can join, because there's no fair way to price the matches already played.

Can I change my pick?

Yes, as often as you like until the match starts. Then picks lock for everyone.

The match was washed out.

The organizer enters "no result" and everyone is refunded for that match.

Why is my balance different from my profit?

Profit counts only matches; your balance also includes the buy-in you paid. Balance = buy-in + profit, and that's what you get back at the end.

Who can see my phone number and UPI id?

Only you, and the organizer of a league or contest you're in.

I didn't get an alert.

Alerts appear inside the app (the bell, with a red badge). There are no emails or phone notifications, so open the app before each match.

The page says "Could not reach the server".

The server may be waking after a quiet spell. Wait up to a minute and refresh.

Who are the admins?

They look after accounts, tournaments and teams, and reset forgotten passwords. They can step in for an organizer only with a written reason, which is kept on record.

Have fun, and pick wisely.Questions about a league or contest? Ask its organizer. Can't sign in? Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page.
